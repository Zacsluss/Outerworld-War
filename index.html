BROOD WAR

Fan remake. Three races, full tech trees, fog of war, AI opponents. No original assets.
Campaign missions
Multiplayer (LAN)
Everyone opens this page from the same server address (see the LAN URL printed by serve.js), connects, and the host presses start.
Left click select, right click command. A attack-move, M move, S stop, H hold, P patrol, B build, V advanced build. Ctrl+1-9 control groups. F1 in-game help, F10 menu, +/- speed.

SETTINGS

Audio starts muted every time the page loads. Unmuting lasts for this session only.
In game: Ctrl+M mutes, F10 opens the pause menu, and Settings there changes the same options.
Tab cycles the selected kind, comma finds an idle worker, Ctrl+A selects the army, Backspace centres on the selection.
